# Competing Risks Models

> Pages: 721

Another important topic is allowing for more than two possible states. Competing risks models allow for the possibility that an individual may exit into different alternatives. For example, a person working full-time may choose to retire completely or work part-time. Han and Hausman (1990) and Sueyoshi (1992) contain discussions of the assumptions needed to estimate competing risks models, with and without unobserved heterogeneity.
