# IINTRODUCTION AND BACKGROUND

> Pages: 20-21

In this part we introduce the basic approach to econometrics taken throughout the book and cover some background material that is important to master before reading the remainder of the text. Students who have a solid understanding of the algebra of conditional expectations, conditional variances, and linear projections could skip Chapter 2, referring to it only as needed. Chapter 3 contains a summary of the asymptotic analysis needed to read Part II and beyond. In Part III we introduce additional asymptotic tools that are needed to study nonlinear estimation.
